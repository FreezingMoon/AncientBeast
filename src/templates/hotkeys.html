Hotkeys

Abilities

  • Q Ability 1 / Deselect
  • W Ability 2
  • E Ability 3
  • R Ability 4
  • Tab Next Ability
  • Shift+Tab Previous Ability

Turn

  • S Skip Turn
  • D Delay Unit
  • Enter / Space Confirm
  • Esc Cancel / Close

Navigation

  • Arrow Keys Move Cursor
  • Ctrl (hold) Show Movement Range

Views

  • T Scoreboard
  • Shift+D Dashboard
  • Shift+A Audio Player
  • Shift+F Fullscreen
  • F11 Fullscreen
  • Backtick Combat Log

Misc

  • A Cycle Audio Mode
  • X Exit Match
  • Ctrl+S Save Combat Log
